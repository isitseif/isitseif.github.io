About

I have been producing since 2018 and releasing with artists across Egypt and the wider MENA region, with over six million streams across platforms. My work sits between hip-hop, R&B, trap, afrobeats and electronic music, and most often I am the one writing the melodies, building the arrangement and mixing the record before it goes out.

Alongside the music I study Media Technology (B.Sc.) at TU Berlin, where I work on acoustics, signal processing and usability research. That side of it shows up in the brand work — understanding why a three-second sonic logo has to survive a phone speaker is a technical problem as much as a creative one.

I grew up between Cairo and Berlin and work in Arabic, German and English.
